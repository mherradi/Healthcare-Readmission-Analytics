# 🏥 Healthcare Readmission Analytics Platform

ApexLife Hospitals operates a large healthcare network across multiple cities, serving thousands of patients every month. One of the major challenges faced by the organization is the growing number of patients being readmitted within 30 days of discharge.

These readmissions create significant financial losses, increase operational pressure on hospitals, and negatively impact patient outcomes and satisfaction.

This project aims to build a modern Data Engineering platform that centralizes healthcare data, identifies readmission patterns, and provides actionable insights to reduce avoidable readmissions.

## 🚨 Business Problem

Hospital readmissions within 30 days are often considered indicators of treatment failure or inadequate post-discharge care.

1️⃣ The primary objective of this project is to create a unified healthcare analytics platform that consolidates patient information from various operational systems into a single source of truth.

2️⃣ Identify patients who are readmitted within thirty days, measure readmission trends across hospitals and departments, and uncover patterns that may contribute to repeated hospital visits.

3️⃣ Establishes a foundation for future predictive models that can identify high-risk patients before readmission occurs.

## 📌 Expected Outcomes

The expected outcome of this project is a data-driven framework that enables ApexLife Hospitals to better understand and reduce patient readmissions. By providing visibility into readmission trends, financial impact, and operational bottlenecks, the platform supports initiatives aimed at improving patient care while reducing unnecessary costs.


## 📋 Conclusion

Beyond reporting, the solution establishes the foundation for predictive healthcare analytics, allowing the organization to proactively identify high-risk patients and intervene before readmission occurs.

The final result is a scalable, structured, and production-ready data platform built using Databricks and Delta Lake
